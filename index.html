How to use the priority tags

  • High practice: concept-rich areas that are useful to practise with mixed PYQs and competency questions.
  • Core: important examinable content; prepare the full chapter, not just the starred topics.
  • Full chapter: complete coverage is required even if no special priority tag is shown.
  • Map / formative: special assessment treatment is noted explicitly from the CBSE curriculum.

Priority is a revision guide, not a prediction or a guarantee of questions.
